CXPalm 0.0.2 / build2: Web and Android can reuse the last newly created chat's notification, model, reasoning effort and permission mode, separately for each computer and client. Full access requires fresh confirmation; approval grants, conversation content and execution ownership are never copied. Settings are applied once through the original desktop owner after connection; an unconfirmed setup retains the created chat and warns instead of replaying creation. Existing configuration, authorizations and startup preferences are preserved.
